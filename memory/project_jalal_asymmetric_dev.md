Jalal reviewed the idea of using dedicated agents to parallelize individual parts of the DCE process (matrix gen, dossier, deliverable gen, etc.) and suggested agents may not be the ideal approach. He used the term "asymmetrical development" as an alternative strategy.

**Why:** Discussion around 2026-05-11 about how to speed up total elapsed time for a full DCE engagement (currently 12–25 min end-to-end).

**How to apply:** When discussing architecture strategy for Harada-Flow, consider both the agent-based parallel approach AND whatever "asymmetrical development" means in this context. Need to explore both before committing to a direction.
